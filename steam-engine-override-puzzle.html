Turning dials is free and doesn't affect the gauges. Gauges only update when you SUBMIT: the closer a digit is to correct (hex distance, wrapping past F), the more its gauge drops toward optimum — the farther off in either direction, the harder it spikes. Pressure is different — it tracks attempts spent, not dial accuracy, and only ever climbs.
